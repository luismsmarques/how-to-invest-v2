3Position size from your risk

Lots = (Balance × Risk%) ÷ (Stop in pips × Pip value in ₹ per lot)
Worked example: ₹1,00,000 account · 1% risk (₹1,000) · 20-pip stop on EUR/USD.
Pip per lot ≈ ₹880  →  1,000 ÷ (20 × 880) = 0.056 → round DOWN to 0.05 lots (5 micro lots) · actual risk ₹880.
Always round down — rounding up silently risks more than you chose. The 1–2% risk figure is a widely cited convention in trading literature, not a rule.

4Forex market hours in IST

India has no daylight saving — IST is fixed at UTC+5:30, so it is the foreign sessions that shift twice a year.
